## Selected engineering work

### [Rover](https://veyoe.com/rover) — autonomous bilingual service robot

A 1.4-meter autonomous service robot that works as a bilingual receptionist, an interactive event kiosk, and a 150 kg delivery courier. Every panel, bracket and gimbal is 3D-printed in-house in Erbil, and the entire software stack — conversation UI, face engine, vision and drive control — was written from scratch.

- **Vision & sensing** — 4 cameras (4K head-tracking, two rear fisheye, internal cargo monitor) and 8 ultrasonic sensors for autonomous navigation. Rover physically turns to hold eye contact.
- **Interaction** — bilingual English/Kurdish AI with 15+ dynamic facial emotions, running unscripted rather than from a fixed dialogue tree.
- **Drivetrain** — repurposed dual-hoverboard drive, built for smooth movement through high-traffic spaces.
- **Cargo** — magnetic, modular compartments that detach tool-free for custom module configurations.

Deployed and running live at public events. **Best School Project**, UKH Convocation Day 2026, judged by the University of Kurdistan Hewlêr's School of Science and Engineering as a complete integrated system. **Honorable Mention**, NICE2026 National Innovation Competition.

### [TIU Assistant](https://veyoe.com/tiuhelp) — conversational AI over a live university database

Ask a question in plain language and get a real answer out of the university's records — not a canned FAQ. The assistant resolves the query, retrieves from a structured institutional database, and returns a rendered result rather than a wall of text.

- **Natural-language retrieval** — covers a staff and faculty directory, office assignments by building and floor, the full academic catalogue (8 faculties, 77 programs — 33 bachelor's and 44 master's), fees, policies, opening hours and admissions questions.
- **Entity resolution** — ask "Where's Dr. Abubakar Ashir's office?" and it identifies the person, then returns a profile card with their title, department, email and room (Main Building – 228).
- **Handoff to 3D navigation** — every result carries a **View on 3D Map** action that opens the campus navigation system below and routes you from where you are to that room. The two systems are built to work as one.
- **Interface** — voice input, dark mode, department and role filters (professor, lecturer, dean, administrative staff), and dedicated tabs for chat, professors, offices, the 3D map and FAQ.

### [TIU Campus Navigation](https://veyoe.com/tiu) — real-time indoor pathfinding in 3D

Interactive indoor navigation built on a 1:1 scale 3D model of the Tishk International University campus, produced in Blender. Pick a start and a destination and it computes the route, the distance and the estimated walking time.

- **Mobility-aware routing** — route preferences for *prefer elevators*, *prefer stairs* or *prefer main stairs*, so the path adapts to how someone actually needs to move through the building.
- **Two view modes** — a first-person walkthrough alongside the overhead map, with room labels and professor information overlaid directly on the 3D environment.
- **Wayfinding overlays** — one-tap lookup for the nearest restroom, elevator or exit, plus overlays for stairs, emergency services and floor transitions.
- **Accessibility** — high-contrast mode, adjustable model quality for lower-end devices, dark/light themes and camera reset.
